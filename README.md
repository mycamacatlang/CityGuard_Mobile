Dagupan City, with a population of over 170,000 residents across 31 barangays,
faces significant challenges in emergency response. Current systems rely heavily on
manual communication and social media, leading to response delays, unverified
information, and coordination difficulties during critical situations such as flooding, road
accidents, medical emergencies, and fire incidents.
To address these challenges, the proponents developed CityGuard, an intelligent
emergency alert and secure incident reporting mobile application for Android. The
platform integrates TensorFlow Lite for AI-powered image classification and
Hyperledger Fabric blockchain for immutable incident logging—technologies selected
for their efficiency in mobile environments and ability to ensure data integrity and
transparency.
Key features developed include geo-tagged emergency reporting, automatic
incident type classification using AI, real-time push notifications to emergency
responders (CDRRMO, PNP, BFP), a centralized responder dashboard, and blockchainsecured incident records that prevent tampering and ensure accountability.
Testing with Dagupan City residents and local emergency units demonstrated that
the AI classification achieved reliable accuracy in identifying emergency types, while the
blockchain logging system successfully prevented unauthorized modifications to incident
records. User acceptance testing confirmed the application's usability and effectiveness in
streamlining emergency reporting workflows.
The project concludes that CityGuard provides a viable solution for improving
Dagupan City's emergency response infrastructure, supporting SDG 11: Sustainable
Cities and Communities by enhancing public safety, disaster resilience, and community
trust in emergency reporting systems.
